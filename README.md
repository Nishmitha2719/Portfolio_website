## so what is this?

this is my corner of the internet. 🪐

move your mouse over my name and watch it fall apart into particles (it comes back, promise). scroll down and the whole universe behind the page keeps changing shape: floating clusters, a neural network, a spinning sphere, waves, a double helix, and finally a glowing portal to say hi.

and there's no React, no Next.js and no build step. it's plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, plus a WebGL shader and a few thousand particles. yes, on purpose. 😌

## things i've built & broken 🛠️

🍃 **Medicinal Leaf Identification**: taught EfficientNet + ArcFace to tell medicinal plants apart from their leaves. it became an **IEEE paper**. 96.18% reported accuracy.

📉 **Fixed Switch Optimizer**: Adam is fast, SGD generalizes better, so why not both? i studied *when* to switch, including a version that switches on its own once the loss stops moving (Dynamic Momentum Control).

📡 **RF Drone Detection** *(research intern @ NITK)*: caught drones in radio noise with a HackRF and taught a model to tell drones from birds and plastic-bodied drones.

🌳 **CanopyCare**: predicting where forests are at risk, with geospatial ML and spatial validation.

🎶 **SwarCare**: an Arduino UNO Q that listens to a Saraswati Veena and checks on its health.

🕸️ **Ripple Effect**: one shaky open-source package can ripple through everything that depends on it, so i used graph ML to predict that risk.

…plus cattle heat-stress detection 🐄, AI dental forensics 🦷 (shortlisted from 300 teams!), CollabHub, an AI image generator and a couple of full-stack web apps.

👉 the site has the full stories, diagrams and all.

## peek under the hood 🔧

```
├── index.html        → the skeleton
├── css/site.css      → all the drip ✨
├── js/
│   ├── data.js       → every word on the site lives here
│   ├── visuals.js    → hand-drawn SVG art for each project
│   ├── background.js → the shader + particle universe 🌌
│   └── app.js        → cursor, filters, charts, timeline, magic
└── assets/           → me 📸
```

**want to change something?** almost everything is in `js/data.js`. add a project, drop in a GitHub link, and the buttons show up on their own.

## it's nice to everyone 💜

- turned on reduced motion? the particles calm down for you
- keyboard friendly all the way through
- animations pause when you can't see them, so no melting laptops 🔥
