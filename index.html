    Study 1

    Published · IEEE

    Medicinal leaf identification with EfficientNet and ArcFace

    Problem

    Many medicinal plants have leaves that look alike, and telling them apart by eye takes expertise that isn't always available. The goal: identify the species reliably from a leaf image.

    Method

    An EfficientNet backbone extracts visual features from each leaf image. Training uses ArcFace, an additive angular-margin objective, which pushes embeddings of different species further apart on the hypersphere so that similar-looking leaves stay separable.

    Data and model

    Input
    Medicinal leaf images
    Backbone
    EfficientNet
    Objective
    ArcFace (angular margin)
    Task
    Species classification

    96.18%

    reported accuracy

    Study 2

    Machine Learning | Optimization | Research

    Fixed Switch Optimizer with Dynamic Momentum Control

    Adam converges fast; SGD often generalizes better. This study asks how to get both by switching from one to the other mid-training, and when that switch should happen.

    Two mechanisms

    • Fixed Switch moves from Adam to SGD at 80% of the training budget.
    • Dynamic Momentum Control triggers the transition when the loss stabilizes, instead of at a predetermined step.

    Compared against

    AdamSGDAdamWSWATSAdaBoundLookaheadand others

    Evaluated on

    MNISTCIFAR-10M4 time series

    Analysis covered convergence behavior, training loss, validation performance and optimization stability.

    Study 3

    Research internship · NITK · May – July 2026

    RF-based aerial drone detection and classification

    Drones talk to their controllers over radio. Capturing that spectrum with a HackRF software-defined radio makes it possible to detect a drone, and to tell it apart from things that look similar to other sensors.

    What I built

    • An RF-based aerial drone detection and classification system using HackRF.
    • An ML pipeline that distinguishes drones, birds and plastic-bodied drones.
    • An extension of that pipeline that identifies the detected drone type.
